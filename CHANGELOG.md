# What's new in SpaceDash

Each release lists what changed for people editing their site with SpaceDash, newest first.

## Unreleased

## 1.4.0 (2026-10-18)

- Blocks on a page start closed, so a page reads as a tidy list of what's on it. Click one to open it; a block you've just added opens by itself.
- Media now shows every image on your site, folder by folder, including your logo and gallery photos. Upload, drag in, make folders, copy an image's address or delete it. Your logo and favicon are marked, and can't be deleted until you choose different ones in Settings.
- Choose a gallery's folder by its photos, or make a new one, instead of typing its path.
- Put an event's date, time and place anywhere in its text with the new Event details block, choosing which of them to show and whether the place links to its map.
- The all-galleries block is now called Photo wall, explains what it shows, and can show just a few photos.
- The real preview works for sites that keep their packages in a folder above the site (such as the SpaceClub starter inside the SpaceClub package).

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-17)

- Make your own content types (Team, Projects...) on the new Content types screen, and add them to any page as a block.
- Every page lives in one Pages list, with its own address and a "Show in the menu" switch.
- Light and dark mode, with a switch in the top bar that follows your computer by default.
- The real preview: see your page as it will look on the site, as you type.

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-16)

- A space theme for the editor, with a SpaceDash login screen.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-16)

- Sites can add their own blocks alongside SpaceDash's.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-16)

- SpaceDash: the editor, added to a site with one script tag.
